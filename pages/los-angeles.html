About Los Angeles

Los Angeles is the largest city in California and the second-largest city in the United States. Known as the entertainment capital of the world, LA is home to Hollywood, beautiful beaches, and a diverse, multicultural population.

Did You Know?

Los Angeles hosted the Summer Olympics twice (1932 and 1984) and will host them again in 2028, making it the third city to host three times.

The Port of Los Angeles is the busiest container port in the United States, handling over 9 million containers annually.
