About California

California, known as the Golden State, is located on the west coast of the United States. With a population of nearly 40 million people, it is the most populous state in the nation and the third largest by area.

From the beaches of Southern California to the towering redwoods of the north, from the Sierra Nevada mountains to the Mojave Desert, California offers incredible diversity in both landscape and culture.

Quick Facts About California

  • Capital: Sacramento
  • Largest City: Los Angeles
  • Statehood: September 9, 1850 (31st state)
  • State Nickname: The Golden State
  • State Colors: Blue and Gold
  • Population: Approximately 39.5 million (2023 est.)

Top Reasons to Visit California

  1. World-famous beaches and coastal cities
  2. Diverse natural landscapes from deserts to mountains
  3. Thriving technology and entertainment industries
  4. Rich cultural heritage and diversity
  5. Excellent weather year-round in many regions
